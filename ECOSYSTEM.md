# Ecosystem map

`convertible-cranium-commander` is the public Justice OS / Commander interface and documentation surface. It is supporting and non-authoritative.

## Boundary

Commander may propose, explain, inspect, and guide. It does not grant authority, fabricate Kernel state or receipts, or replace the private Kernel authority plane. The quantum-hybrid architecture described here is a design and capability target, not a claim of an audited live quantum runtime.

The private Kernel repository is held by `worthwylfoundation-hue`; the exact authority revision and verification notes are recorded in [`KERNEL_PIN`](./KERNEL_PIN).
